YOUR DATA, YOUR BUSINESS

A little privacy note.

This website has no user accounts, analytics tools, advertising pixels, newsletter subscriptions, or server-side data collection built into its code. It is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site.

What your browser stores

The heart button stores the IDs of your saved shopping ideas in your browser's local storage, under the key pickpop-saved. This helps you see saved items on return visits. It is not sent to a pickpop. server. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

External sites

When you choose "View on Amazon" or "Search on Amazon," your browser leaves this site. Amazon controls the privacy and cookie practices on its own pages. Product affiliate links include the site's public Associates tag, which identifies the referral source; it is not a visitor account identifier. We do not set Amazon affiliate cookies on PickPop. Review its privacy information separately. The typefaces are hosted with this site; your browser does not request them from Google.

Hosting

Netlify hosts this static site and may process connection and technical access information under its own policies. No external analytics service or advertising pixel is enabled in PickPop.
