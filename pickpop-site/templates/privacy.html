YOUR DATA, YOUR BUSINESS

A little privacy note.

This starter website has no user accounts, analytics tools, advertising pixels, newsletter subscriptions, or server-side data collection built into its code. It is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site.

What your browser stores

The heart button stores the IDs of your saved shopping ideas in your browser's local storage, under the key pickpop-saved. This helps you see saved items on return visits. It is not sent to a pickpop. server. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

External sites

When you choose "Search on Amazon," your browser leaves this site. Amazon controls the privacy and cookie practices on its own pages. Review its privacy information separately. The typefaces are hosted with this site; your browser does not request them from Google.

Hosting

Your chosen web host may collect access logs or other technical data in accordance with its own policies. Before publishing, review the selected host's policies, configure any added analytics or cookie notices appropriately, and update this policy to reflect what you actually deploy.

Contact

No public contact address has been configured in this demo yet. Add a real business contact method before public launch.
