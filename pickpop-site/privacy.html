YOUR DATA, YOUR BUSINESS

A little privacy note.

This starter website has no user accounts, analytics tools, advertising pixels, newsletter subscriptions, or application-level server-side data collection built into its code. It is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site.

What your browser stores

The heart button stores the IDs of your saved shopping ideas in your browser's local storage, under the key pickpop-saved. You can turn off “Remember my favorites” in the finder. This removes stored favorite IDs; current-tab favorites remain only for that visit. A small preference key, pickpop-persistence, remembers your choice. Clearing site data resets the choice. It is not sent to a pickpop. server. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

External sites

When you choose "Search on Amazon," your browser leaves this site. Amazon controls the privacy and cookie practices on its own pages. Review its privacy information separately. The typefaces are hosted with this site; your browser does not request them from Google.

Hosting

Your chosen web host may collect access logs or other technical data in accordance with its own policies. Before publishing, review the selected host's policies, configure any added analytics or cookie notices appropriately, and update this policy to reflect what you actually deploy.

Contact

See our contact page for the current contact status. No contact form or third-party submission service is active.
